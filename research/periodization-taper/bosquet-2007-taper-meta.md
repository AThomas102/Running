# Bosquet et al. 2007 — Taper meta-analysis

## Key findings

- Meta-analysis of 27 studies in competitive athletes with defined taper interventions and performance outcomes.
- Most efficient pattern identified: about a **2-week** taper with training **volume reduced exponentially by ~41–60%**.
- Maintain **training intensity** and **training frequency** (prefer shortening sessions over dropping many sessions).
- Progressive/exponential volume reduction outperformed simple step reductions in the synthesis.

## Limitations

- Mixed sports; not runners-only.
- Individual responses vary; very low-volume athletes may need less formal taper.

## Planning implications

- For Andrew’s **HM 15 Nov 2026**: plan ~**10–14 days** taper with **~40–60% volume cut**, keep some sharpness (strides / short quality), do not make everything slow junk miles only.
- Place longest long runs **before** the taper window.
- After the race: separate **recovery/transition** weeks (not the same as taper).

# Achilles midportion loading protocols — 2023 meta-analysis

## Key findings

- 12 RCTs (n = 543) comparing loading protocols and/or passive modalities for midportion Achilles tendinopathy.
- Passive modalities sometimes reduced pain more than eccentric loading in the **short term**; function trends favored loading at some follow-ups without always reaching significance.
- Comparisons **between different loading protocols** (e.g. eccentric vs other loading) showed **no clear superiority** for pain/function across short/mid/long term.
- Authors still support progressive loading (eccentric or heavy slow resistance concentric/eccentric) per clinical guidelines; research should refine dose parameters (load, speed, time under tension).

## Limitations

- Risk of bias concerns in included trials.
- Does not prescribe exact return-to-run mileage ladders.

## Planning implications

- Continue **progressive tendon loading** via S&C; do not obsess over one branded protocol.
- Pair loading with sensible run progression (avoid session spikes that overload the tendon).
- Short-term pain relief from passive care does not replace long-term loading + graded running.

# Silbernagel & Crossley 2015 — Achilles return-to-sport

## Key findings

- Presents a structured **return-to-sport** progression for midportion Achilles tendinopathy alongside continued rehab loading.
- Guiding principle: progressively increase tendon demand by controlling **intensity, duration, and frequency** of loading.
- Suggests athletes have **minimal pain (≈1–2/10)** with ADLs before returning to running/jumping activities.
- Continue daily rehab exercises even on run days during return phase.
- Literature return-to-sport success rates after nonoperative care vary widely; symptom state when returning is often poorly reported.

## Limitations

- Proposed program / clinical rationale — not a large RCT of the exact RTS ladder.
- Focused on midportion Achilles; athlete’s “foot tendonitis” may differ anatomically — principles still useful.

## Planning implications

- Progress run volume/intensity only when morning symptoms and in-run pain stay acceptable.
- Prefer **fewer hard stimuli** and controlled long-run growth (see also session-spike evidence).
- Keep S&C / calf–foot loading as part of the plan, not only more km.
- If pain rises with a session spike, cut duration/intensity/frequency before pushing through.

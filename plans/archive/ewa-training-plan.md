### **Ewa's Training Plan for Great South Run**

**Important Notes:**
Particular focus on running form during the hills, try to stand up tall and avoid over striding. Keep the leg turnover high and controlled, this might mean shortening your stride to keep the same effort.
Easy means EASY! If you can keep your heart rate below 150 bpm that would be amazing, that might even mean some fast walking up the hills!

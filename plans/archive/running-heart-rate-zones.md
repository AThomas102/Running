# Running heart rate zones

Converted from image `Pasted image 20231015212814.png`.

| Zone | % of max HR | Exertion level | Fitness goal |
|------|-------------|----------------|--------------|
| Zone 1 | 50–60% | Very light | Very comfortable effort used for warm ups or cool downs and recovery runs. |
| Zone 2 | 60–70% | Light | Long slow distances and easy runs. You should be able to carry on a conversation in this zone. |
| Zone 3 | 70–80% | Moderate / aerobic | Tempo runs, hill repeats, threshold training. You should be able to speak in sentences. |
| Zone 4 | 80–90% | Hard / anaerobic / lactate threshold | Track workouts, speed intervals, fartlek runs, and 5k race pace. You can't speak in sentences at this pace. |
| Zone 5 | 90–100% | Very hard / anaerobic / VO2 max | Sprints. You won't be able to speak at this pace. |

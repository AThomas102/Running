**starting 16 October 2023**
**easy pace**: Zone 2 60-70% max heart rate, I saw it at 195bpm on strava so I shall take that as your maximum.
So below 140 bpm.
**tempo pace**: Zone 3 70-80% max heart rate.
Between 140-160 bpm. From my experience its ok if it goes up to 170, but not much more.
**interval pace**: Zone 4/5 Running hard but still comfortably. This should be at your current 5k pace. e.g 4:00 /km.
**strides**: fit these in anywhere in the run, slow jog recover between each one.
**Stretching & Warm-up**: ALWAYS stretch and warm-up before tempo, intervals and strides; this will significantly help avoid injuries. Do atleast 10-15min easy pace or slower for warmup beforehand and do a couple static then dynamic exercises.

Its very important to focus on running form! you need to do your own research on this. 
But important concepts include foot landing position, avoiding over stride, cadence etc.
